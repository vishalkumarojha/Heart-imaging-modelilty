# Ten Candidate Titles (ranked)

All titles are consistent with the completed experiments; none claim
state-of-the-art performance, clinical diagnosis, or a novel architecture.

| rank | title |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Calibration-Aware Decision Policies for Multi-Label Chest X-Ray Classification Under Class Imbalance |
| 2 | How Threshold Policies, Not Temperature, Drive F1 in an Imbalanced Multi-Label Chest X-Ray Classifier |
| 3 | Operating-Point Selection for a Frozen DenseNet121 Chest X-Ray Classifier: An Ablation of Calibration versus Thresholds |
| 4 | Separating Calibration from Threshold Effects in Imbalanced Multi-Label Chest Radiograph Classification |
| 5 | Reproducible Decision Policies for a Fixed DenseNet121 Cardiomegaly and Effusion Classifier |

| 10 | Benchmarking Decision Policies, Not Architectures, for Fixed-Weight Chest X-Ray Classifiers |

**Ranking rationale**

- #1 is selected for the paper body: it is accurate, non-overclaiming, and
  matches the measured contribution (decision policies) without claiming
  calibration or threshold novelty.
- #2–#3 are informative but more editorial; they read like results-led abstracts.
- #4–#5 emphasize the ablation/contribution split but are narrower.
- #6 names ECE/Brier, which the paper reports transparently but does not claim
  to fix.
- #7–#10 are alternatives with varying emphasis on reproducibility, ablation,
  and class imbalance; #10 is discouraged because it could be misread as an
  architecture benchmark.

**Selected title (see `abstract.md` and `paper_outline.md`):**

> **Calibration-Aware Decision Policies for Multi-Label Chest X-Ray
> Classification Under Class Imbalance**

The selected title does not mention external validation, which was not
completed.
